# About character animation

The About hero uses transparent artwork directly on the page, with separate CSS spark/dot accents and a clay italic headline finish. The image-generation skill supplied the cutout and motion artwork through the built-in image tool (not the CLI). The desktop illustration is capped at 26rem and takes 88% of its grid column; mobile uses a centered composition capped at 24rem and 80% of the content width.

## Rendering and behavior

`components/about-character.tsx` draws the opening sheet region [100, 0, 300, 418] as the permanent foundation. Generated column spacing is 384 pixels and row spacing is 418 pixels; do not infer the column offsets from the sheet width. Only small eye/hand patches are composited from frames 1, 2, 4, 5 (zero-based). The hand patches have explicit registration offsets and two-pixel edge feathering to match the fixed wrist and dress/podium seam. There is no body scale change, crossfade between full poses, or floating motion. The larger weight-shift idea was omitted to keep contact points and silhouette steady.

After the artwork loads and enters view, the idle sequence starts after 150ms: the first visible blink frame appears at about 240ms and the fingers begin moving at about 850ms. Blink and two gentle finger gestures take 1.93 seconds, followed by a short 1.2–1.8 second rest. Idle rendering uses timers only at pose changes. It resets to the resting pose when offscreen or the tab is hidden. Reduced motion disables idle gestures. CSS supplies the no-JavaScript/canvas fallback. The decorative star uses the same `--ochre` yellow as the home hero.

## Interactive toolkit

`components/about-toolkit.tsx` wraps the hero copy and character and owns a native modal dialog. The “Psst—see what I build with” prompt and a transparent button over the illustrated laptop both trigger the opening. The original character artwork and idle patches remain intact; the star sits to the character's right to leave the arrow clear.

The lid is isolated from the original raster and projected around a fixed hinge on each animation frame, using two texture triangles and a foreground mask for the original forearm. Opening takes 560ms (roughly 34 frames at 60Hz); closing takes 360ms. The removal mask includes the raised lid's antialiased fringe but stops about three source pixels above the fixed hinge. A separate exclusion clip protects the original podium corner and front rim from both clearing and the moving lid texture, so their pixels remain untouched. There are no replacement backing or podium-fill patches. Texture triangles overlap only inside the lid silhouette, preventing protruding corners. The body and podium are never replaced by a generated rectangular crop. Canvas rendering is at 2× resolution. The arrow hides as soon as opening starts and returns on close. The laptop hotspot has no hover tint or shadow; only its pointer cursor changes (keyboard focus retains an outline). The modal appears after 660ms, without scrolling the page, and uses warm paper colors, ink borders, and a flat dock instead of a second laptop shell.

Selecting any of the twelve unframed app logos shows its name, category, and description. GitHub completes the last row and uses Primer's official `mark-github-24` icon. Hover/focus labels and neighboring-icon magnification support desktop exploration; small screens use three even rows of four with the selected app's name and explanation above the logos. Intermediate tablet widths use two rows of six. The mobile dialog has a compact heading and sticky close bar, and its height accounts for viewport safe areas. The invitation and arrow scale with the illustration on narrow screens. Native modal semantics keep background content inert and focus inside the dialog. Close, Escape, and an outside tap restore the initiating control and close the illustrated lid. Backdrop dismissal requires both pointer-down and click outside the dialog bounds, so dragging out from inside does not accidentally close it. The body scroll lock is removed on close/unmount. The prompt floats only until first use. Reduced motion skips lid interpolation, modal reveal, and dock scaling. A no-JavaScript text list preserves the tool names.
